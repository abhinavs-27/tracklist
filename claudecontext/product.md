# Product

## Core Value Prop

A passive social network for music taste:

- Automatically track everything you listen to (via Last.fm)
- Turn listening history into identity, not just data
- Discover music through people and communities

## Key Differentiators

- Zero-effort logging (no manual input required)
- Taste identity built from real listening behavior
- Community-driven charts and consensus
- Cross-platform listening aggregation (Spotify, etc. via Last.fm)

## Target User

- Music enthusiasts already using Last.fm
- Users who want a deeper identity layer on top of listening
- People who care about patterns, not just individual tracks

## Core Loops

1. Listen to music → auto-ingested → appears in feed
2. See friends’ activity → discover → listen → repeat
3. Join communities → compare taste → increase engagement

## Success Metrics

- Daily active users with recent ingestion
- % of users with successful Last.fm sync
- Feed engagement rate
- Community participation rate
- Retention (D7, D30)

## Risks

- Users without Last.fm cannot onboard
- Ingestion delays reduce perceived responsiveness
- Data inaccuracies (missing or wrong listens)
- Weak differentiation if it feels like a Last.fm wrapper

## Non-Goals

- Not a manual tracking app
- Not competing with Spotify as a player
- Not focused on casual listeners
