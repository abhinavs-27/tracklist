01 — Profile Banner

Profile Hero Banner

The band of album art at the top of every profile. Currently a flat 4-image horizontal strip. Two directions below.

What changes: 2-row mosaic, second row offset by half a tile so edges never align. Taller at 160px (up from 144px). Side vignettes prevent hard cutoffs at the edges. Content below the banner sits naturally on the page — no extra card container.

When no albums: Deep gradient with a subtle radial glow instead of a flat zinc slab.

02 — Home Tabs

Home Tab Bar

The Billboard / Pulse / History / Activity tabs. Currently a plain underline style. Three directions — each changes the visual weight of the active state.

Option A — Filled pill Recommended
Active pill: opaque zinc fill with ring. No underline needed.
Why this works: The filled pill reads immediately — no need to hunt for a thin underline. Consistent with how iOS and many modern apps signal selected state. The segmented shell itself adds depth to an otherwise flat area of the page.
Option B — Bold underline Alternative
Same structure as today — bolder font weight on active, thicker gold line.
Minimal change: This is the current design with slightly heavier typography and a thicker (2px) indicator line. Lowest risk — familiar and easy to scan. Less visually exciting but never wrong.
Option C — Tab with context line Alternative
Your weekly ranked chart · May 19–25
A small context line under the tab strip contextualizes what you're looking at.
Adds context: A single-line subtitle updates dynamically per active tab (e.g. "Your weekly ranked chart · May 19–25" on Billboard, "Rolling 7-day stats" on Pulse). Gives first-time users a clue about what each tab holds.
03 — Billboard Drop Banner

Billboard Drop Banner

The notification shown at the top of the home page when a new weekly chart is ready. Currently a subtle amber-tinted card.

Option A — Prominent announcement card Recommended
Weekly Billboard
Your chart for May 19–25 is ready
See what rose, what dropped, and your #1 this week.
View chart
What changes: The icon is now a small bar-chart SVG (no emoji). Layout switches to three-part: icon · text · CTA. The CTA is larger and gold, not amber-text on dark. The glow underneath is more dramatic — this should feel like a real weekly drop event.
Option B — Compact notification strip Alternative
Your Billboard for May 19–25 is ready
Weekly ranked chart  ·  new entries this week
More restrained: A left-accent bar (common in notification/alert UI) instead of a large glow. Takes up far less vertical space. Better if you expect users to dismiss it quickly and move on — doesn't compete visually with the chart content below it.
