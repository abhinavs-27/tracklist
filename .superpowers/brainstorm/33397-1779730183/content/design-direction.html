A — Editorial Dark

Bold serif headlines, magazine-style typography, rich accent color (crimson/deep red or another non-Spotify hue). Still dark, but feels like Pitchfork or a high-end music publication — not a streaming app. Identity comes from typography and color confidence, not brightness.

B — Art-Reactive

Album art drives the whole page — extract its dominant colors and use them for ambient glows, accent colors, rating UI tinting, and tab indicators. Every album page feels unique. This is the biggest "wow" moment in modern music apps (similar to Apple Music's latest design). Requires a color-extraction library on the server.

C — Warm & Playful

Replaces Spotify's green with a warm orange-amber palette. Adds subtle personality touches — small emoji labels, gradient CTA buttons, rounded pill tags. Stays dark but feels warmer, more human. Closest to Tapehead's vibe. Easiest to implement since it's mostly a color/personality shift without needing dynamic extraction.

These aren't mutually exclusive — we can combine directions (e.g., B's art-reactive glow + C's warmth). But pick the one that feels most right as a starting point.
