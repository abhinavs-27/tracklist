How it works in code: The same extractAlbumPalette() we use for the chart card runs on each of the top 3 artist images. Artist 1's color → primary blob (top-right, strongest). Artist 2's color → secondary blob (bottom-left). Artist 3's color → tertiary subtle center. The three radial gradients overlap and blend naturally. Real artist photos produce much more varied and interesting palettes than these simulated ones — a Radiohead listener will get something totally different from a Bad Bunny listener even within the same genre bucket.
