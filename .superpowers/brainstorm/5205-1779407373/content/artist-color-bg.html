How the color extraction works:

Each artist's photo is fetched → dominant color extracted → the three artist colors become three radial gradient blobs (primary at top-right, secondary at bottom-left, tertiary subtle center). The blobs overlap and blend naturally. Result: a background that's atmospherically yours — a hip-hop listener gets moody purples, an electronic listener gets cool teals, a pop listener gets warm pinks. Same as the chart card's color extraction, just sourced from all three artists instead of one album.
