Listening Style Label Redesign

Current labels on the left, proposed replacements on the right. The label is the hero of the shareable card — it needs to feel like an identity, not a description.

Current
Proposed
Mainstream
You gravitate toward popular music — the stuff that's already everywhere.
Cultural Pulse
Always plugged into what the world is listening to. You know what's next before it's everywhere.
Condition: high avg track popularity (>64)
Deep cuts
You reach for tracks that don't show up on the front page.
The Archivist
You dig past the surface. Your picks rarely appear on anyone else's radar.
Condition: low avg track popularity (<48)
On Loop
A few records get most of the plays — you go deep on what you love.
Full Album Energy
You commit. A handful of records run deep — replayed until you know every breath.
Condition: low album-to-log ratio, repeating albums
Omnivore
Lots of different artists — hard to pin to one lane.
Genre Nomad
No borders. You move freely across genres, artists, eras — always following the sound.
Condition: high unique artists + high diversity rate
Mainstay
Most plays go to a tight circle of long-term favorites.
The Loyalist
Your artists earned their spot. A tight circle, kept for a reason — you don't stray easily.
Condition: high top-artist share, lower diversity
Consistent
You listen regularly without wild spikes or gaps — just steady rotation.
Daily Ritual
Music is baked into your day. Not a mood, not a phase — just always on.
Condition: steady daily play count, no extreme spikes
Deep sessions
Some days you stack a lot of plays in a single sitting.
Session Maximalist
When you're in it, you're really in it. Some sessions run for hours — full immersion.
Condition: high max logs/day or very high daily average
Still building
Keep logging listens and your style will start to take shape.
Just Getting Started
Your sound is taking shape. Log more listens and your identity will emerge.
Condition: <22 total logs

Key changes: "Mainstream" → "Cultural Pulse" (removes the negative connotation — being plugged in is cool). "Mainstay" → "The Loyalist" (loyalty is a virtue). "Consistent" → "Daily Ritual" (a habit sounds intentional). "Still building" → "Just Getting Started" (welcoming, not dismissive). Each label now has a distinctive accent color for the shareable card.
